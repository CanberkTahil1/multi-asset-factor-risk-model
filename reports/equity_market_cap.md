## The pre-XBRL approximation (row 270)

**13.44%** of the (session, name) pairs with a cap in the estimation universe carry a BACK-FILLED count: the name's earliest filed count, split-adjusted, in force on sessions before that filing. Direction: buybacks between the session and the first filing mean the true count was HIGHER, so the back-fill UNDERSTATES those early caps; net issuance means it OVERSTATES them. For the largest filers the gap runs from the sample start to their first cover-page fact in 2009; smaller filers phase in through 2011. Every consumer of `cap` can read the mask (`MarketCapPanel.approximated`) and every equity report states this share.

## Ruling (i): the within-name consistency screen (SPEC.md 15.4.3, rows 274-275)

`R = 100`. A filed count more than R times the name's own median split-adjusted count, or below 1/R of it, is dropped; the previous count stays in force; the back-fill reads the earliest surviving count. R is not tuned. On the median-relative ratios the screen acts on (count / the name's median, folded to >= 1, every usable filing among universe names, unscreened), the 2026-09-05 pull leaves (55, 488) empty and R sits inside it; `tests/test_market_cap_dataset.py::test_r_sits_inside_the_measured_empty_band` recomputes the band around R on every re-pull, prints its ends and asserts it still contains this one, and `test_no_dropped_count_is_a_corporate_action` asserts that no dropped ratio is one the actions table explains. A future filing inside the band fails the first test and RE-OPENS R with numbers. (W7-P2's first statement of the band, (10, 1000) on consecutive-count ratios, was a misreading; SPEC.md 15.4.3.)

## What this is not

- Not float-adjusted: the fact is total common shares outstanding on the cover page, so caps are total, not free-float, and the cap weights of closely held names are larger than an index provider's.
- Not per class: the frames carry only the non-dimensional total, so a multi-class filer either contributes its total to every listed class or, when it reports per class, nothing at all (`no_facts`).
- Not complete before 2009: the pre-XBRL back-fill above is an approximation and is labelled as one everywhere the panel is read.
- The survivorship finding of SPEC.md 15.3.2 stands underneath all of this: the universe on 2007-04-02 is what Yahoo still serves, and the names without a cap on that session are overwhelmingly departures that never filed the XBRL fact.
