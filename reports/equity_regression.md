## The regression

USE4 Eq. 3.1: `r_n = f_c + sum_i X_ni f_i + sum_s X_ns f_s + u_n` by weighted least squares with `w_n` proportional to `cap_n^0.5` normalised to one -- MSCI's stated assumption that specific variance is inversely proportional to sqrt(cap); not cap-weighted, not equal-weighted (CLAUDE.md failure mode 8) -- under the CAP-weighted industry sum-to-zero constraint `sum_i w_i f_i = 0` (Eq. 3.3) through a K x (K-1) matrix R built on the industries present that date, eliminating the largest-cap one. Industries: **Fama-French 49 mapped from EDGAR SIC codes** (`https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/ftp/Siccodes49.zip`). GICS is proprietary and is arguably the single largest contributor to Barra's explanatory power, so this substitution costs more than it appears to: FF49 has no notion of a company's business mix, assigns one SIC-based industry per name, and leaves many of its 49 near-empty on a ~400-name universe (below). Thin-industry rule: **none** (SPEC.md 15.6.1 ruling 3) -- an absent industry's return is NaN, a one-member industry is estimated as-is.

**Regression set.** The estimation universe minus the no-cap names (dual-class filers and BRK-B, `no_facts`), whose share of universe DOLLAR VOLUME on 2024-12-31 is **8.76%** (28 names; a proxy, because the cap is what they lack), minus names without a SIC (ruling 2; 0 in the universe: none), a complete set of exposures, or a return. Per date: 305-458 names (mean 379), of 307-460 with a cap; excluded on the mean date -- no SIC 0.0, no exposure 2.3, no return 0.0. Two known residuals from W7-P2, stated and not fixed here: 2.2% of universe cells carry splits the actions table lacks, and every pre-first-filing count is a labelled approximation (`reports/equity_market_cap.md`).

## The two constraint tests

On every one of the 4468 dates: (a) the cap-weighted sum of the present industries' factor returns is zero to **2.60e-18** at most (tolerance 1e-10); (b) the identity `c'r = f_c + sum_i w_i f_i + sum_s (c'x_s) f_s + c'u` over the regression set closes to **2.78e-17** at most. The country factor is the cap-weighted market return of the regression set **minus the market's specific return** `c'u` (plus a residual style term, since the regression set is a subset of the standardization set): exact under cap weights, and under the sqrt-cap weights the spec mandates exact only up to `c'u` (SPEC.md 15.6.1, derived before the run). Measured: corr(f_c, c'r) = **0.9998**; corr(f_c, estimation-universe market_excess) = **0.9998**; the gap `c'r - f_c` has daily sd **2.4 bp** against the market's 127.2 bp, ratio **0.018**; its mean is +0.02 bp/day, of which the style term contributes +0.007 bp/day and the specific term +0.01 bp/day.

## Industries: coverage and SIC drift (SPEC.md 15.6.1 rulings 1-2)

Of the 539 estimation-universe names with a screened cap, **539 (100.0%) have a current SIC** through EDGAR's submissions record and hence an FF49 industry; 0 have a CIK but no SIC (excluded, never 'Other'), 0 no CIK, 0 not delivered by the pull. **4 (0.7%) fall in 49 'Other'**: AES, AMCR, RSG, WM. Drift: among the 538 names with both a current SIC and a first in-sample 10-K header SIC, **41 (7.6%) changed SIC code and 27 (5.0%) changed FF49 industry**; 0 names have no 10-K on or after `sample.start`, 1 a header without an ASSIGNED-SIC, 0 not delivered. The names whose industry differs are listed in `reports/equity_industries.md`; the current SIC is applied backwards by ruling 2, and whether the drift is 'small' is the operator's call on these numbers.

## The point-in-time correction (SPEC.md 15.6.3, W7-P3b)

For the 27 universe names whose FF49 industry differs between the first in-sample 10-K and now (and every other mapped name the two-point check flags), every in-sample 10-K header was pulled (591 in-sample filings: {'ok': 591}) and the SIC is KNOWN from its filing date, forward-filled to the next filing and back-filled to `sample.start` with the first filing's SIC (labelled). The other names keep the current code. The rule changed the industry of **29412 of 1692791 regression cells (1.74%)**, for **16 names**; the other drifted names changed industry before they entered the estimation universe, so their early-sample code never reached a regression. The residual the two-point check cannot see, named and not pulled: a change-and-revert between two 10-Ks of an unchanged name.
